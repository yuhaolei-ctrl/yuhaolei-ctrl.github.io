About

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Carnegie Mellon University.

My advisor is Prof. Matthew Rosenzweig. I have also worked with Prof. Mykhaylo Shkolnikov, Prof. Konstantin Tikhomirov, and Prof. Zhenfu Wang (BICMR).

My research interests are in probability theory and stochastic systems. Specifically, I study Coulomb gases, including β-ensembles and log gases, and the functional inequalities related to them. I also study mean field limits and propagation of chaos for large stochastic interacting particle systems, as well as randomized algorithms.
